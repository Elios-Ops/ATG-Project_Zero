## Executive assessment

ZELTAR upgrades the validated Pod Racer release in a separate working copy. It retains the original race, scoring, multiplayer/leaderboard integration, safeguards and tower geometry. COMET, VIPER and TITAN are original ZelPods generated by the existing ATG surface-model pipeline, not purchased assets or franchise designs. The ZelPod Design Lab, selection, race HUD, result screen and experience metadata now use the ZELTAR identity. The architectural release is named The ZEL Towers, with All Things Gamed as the parent brand.

Ready for owner review and a separately approved publication attempt. This is not a claim of live approval.

## What changed

- COMET: swept nose, bevelled hull, low streamlined cockpit, long twin engine bodies, intake turbines, exhaust throats, cooling elements and swept stabilizers.
- VIPER: spear-shaped angular hull, taller wedge cockpit, short compact thrusters, wing outriggers and aggressive stabilizing fins.
- TITAN: broader armored hull, reinforced cockpit collar/rails, armored propulsion units, ring bands, hardpoints, mechanical fasteners and sensor mast.
- Each machine is a connected, complete design with paintable panels, metal frame, faceted opaque cockpit glazing, exhaust/lighting and removable fins. Five material-group meshes, four materials, no textures. Baked vertex shading and unlit runtime materials retain a cheap renderer.
- Three models share five paint choices, three light choices and optional fins: 90 free configurations. Their colors are independent of their handling.
- Former handling profiles are preserved exactly: COMET uses former COMET, VIPER uses former KITE, TITAN uses former VECTOR. No new speed, acceleration, turning, collision or scoring values were introduced.
- The local exterior copy changes the sign atlas to ZELTAR / ZELPOD LAB and the GLB root name. Every geometry accessor, material, shader and lot.json configuration matches the prior release. Exterior LOD0/1/2 remain 1,526 / 614 / 518 triangles. The approved original files and package remain intact.
- Remote visitors now use an original ZelPod stand-in instead of boxes. Geometry is shared across visitors/reconnects. Their chosen vehicle/cosmetics are not transmitted in this time-trial release.
- Result HUD now shows the final gate as 12/12 and removes the boost readout from the leaderboard panel. The Lab displays the time-to-points conversion.

## Performance comparison

Same Chromium SwiftShader software renderer; sequential official devkit.review(15) runs at 960×540, pixel ratio capped at 1, two visitors. Phone samples used 390×844 with six seconds of racing. No hardware GPU or physical phone was used.

| Measurement | Previous validated release | ZELTAR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Desktop review FPS | 39.3 | 43.5 |
| First desktop frame (ms) | 4.9 | 0.5 |
| Worst desktop frame (ms) | 277.5 | 109.4 |
| Phone race FPS | 54 | 56 |
| Worst phone frame (ms) | 50.9 | 59.2 |

This sample shows no performance regression despite more geometry. It is a short comparison, not a statistically established improvement or 60 FPS hardware guarantee. A separate final preflight of the exact final source measured 38.1 FPS desktop and 47 FPS phone; the full-race sample measured 52 FPS. The final preflight's worst frame was 332 ms, with no errors, watchdog stop or sandbox violations. Software desktop samples varied from 38.1 to 43.5 FPS; this variability is a remaining performance measurement limitation, not proof of a speed improvement.

Pixel ratio remains capped at 1 and falls to .65 after four frames above 70 ms. The safeguard test passed. Models are merged by material; there are no shadow maps, post-processing, particles, heavy physics, textures/mipmaps or per-frame model allocation. First-screen geometry/materials are prewarmed using compileAsync.

## Gameplay contract

Three laps, 12 gates per lap, ordered crossings. WASD/arrows: throttle, brake, steering; Shift/touch BOOST: energy-limited boost; R/touch RETRY: reset; B/touch LAB: return to customization; I: platform invitation; Esc: exit. Barrier hit adds one second. Score:

`max(1, 100000 - round(100 × (race seconds + penalty seconds)))`

Higher points win. Scores are sent once after a real finish and at least 10 seconds of session time. Local best and free cosmetics last for the visit; the platform weekly leaderboard is persistent when available. All options are free. No p0.buy, p0.win or paid/prize activation.
